My Everyday

Fair tidings to you, reader! I'm grateful for your visit to my internet home, so to speak. In this section, I wanted to offer an overview of the pursuits I engage in on an average day, so as to provide a glimpse into my professional self.

  • My main commitment is to take end-to-end responsibility for the piece I own in the project, ensuring I deliver easy-to-read code that is integrated with the existing application, along with clear documentation.
  • I also engage in discussions with stakeholders to review progress and to align on the product roadmap.
  • One of my favourite tasks involves working with members across teams to identify state-of-the-art models that we can leverage, deploying them with scripts in ad-hoc scenarios.
  • I take it upon myself to interact with new members and interns to understand their background, identify gaps that can inhibit their progress, and pass on the metaphorical torch of the project.
